The apartment

A mountain hideaway for two

Le Duplex takes up the annex of a wood-and-stone chalet, with its own entrance, in a quiet residential area of Saint-François-Longchamp 1450.

On the ground floor, a bedroom with a 160 cm bed and a shower room. Upstairs, the kitchenette and a small living room with a 140 cm sofa bed. The ski lifts are less than 250 m away: snow permitting, you can ski in and ski out.

Amenities

Everything you need, nothing you don't

  • Ski in, ski out

    Les Clochettes ski lifts less than 250 m away, ski-in/ski-out access depending on snow.

  • Bedroom and shower room

    Separate bedroom with a 160 cm bed, shower room with double sink and WC.

  • Kitchenette

    Everything you need to cook, raclette and crêpe makers, Dolce Gusto coffee machine.

  • Small living room

    Sitting area with a 140 cm sofa bed and TV, mountain and valley views.

  • Linen and wifi

    Sheets and towels provided, wifi throughout.

  • Arrival and parking

    Self check-in with a key box, free parking 10 m from the door.

Good to know

Minimum stay of 3 nights. A €1,000 deposit cheque (not cashed) is requested on arrival. Departure cleaning is up to you, or €60 is kept from the deposit.
